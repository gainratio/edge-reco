EdgeReco

On-device search and recommendations

EdgeReco moves product search, ranking, and recommendations out of a paid backend and into each shopper's browser. A store publishes one signed catalog bundle; the browser verifies it, stores it locally, and answers queries from SQLite with FTS5 and vector search.

What runs locally

  • Keyword search with SQLite FTS5
  • Vector similarity with sqlite-vector
  • Session-aware ranking and recommendations
  • Offline operation after the first sync

Why it matters

No per-query server cost, no catalog API latency, and no upload of shopper behavior. The engine is open source and the bundle is content-addressed and signature-verified.
